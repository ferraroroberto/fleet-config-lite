# slides · content directives

The copy rules for every deck this skill builds. The register: **executive-clear in the strategy-consulting family, deliberately NOT formal-consultant** — direct, warm, concrete.

- **One accent phrase per title** (`<span class="hl">`), never the whole title.
- **Lead with the payoff, mechanisms after.** The title and thesis carry the "so what"; how it works comes below or in a reveal.
- **Small honest numbers beat big asserted ones.** A slide that claims measurement must show figures.
- **Placeholders for unverified figures:** `{{TOKEN}}` for every figure not yet verified, with a fill-in inventory table per deck (token, slide, what fills it, who confirms it).
- **House punctuation:** middot (`·`) joins short items; **no em dashes** — commas, or a period when a comma would stack.
- **Reveal panels carry "why it matters" depth**, never content required to understand the slide.
- **One idea per slide.** If the briefing gives a slide two ideas, it becomes two slides (or the second idea moves to a reveal).
- **`&nbsp;` between digits and their nouns** so numbers never separate from what they count.
- **Audience vocabulary only** — no internal jargon, no presenter mechanics.
- **Bilingual rule:** the briefing's language (ES/EN) drives the deck's language; the skill's machinery, file names and anchors stay English.

## Fill-in guidance for tokens

- Outcome lines: at most 2 wrapped lines once filled.
- Cost strings: ≤14 characters.
- State cards: single numbers only.
